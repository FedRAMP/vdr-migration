# Corrective action plans

For each required VDR/VER rule that is not fully implemented, include a corrective action plan and target timeline in that rule's Mini-SDR response. Explain what remains to be done and how and when you plan to complete it.

The rule-specific plans form the basis of the overall Corrective Action Plan, which can be generated from the Mini-SDR. Do not prepare a separate migration CAP attachment.

A justified departure from a SHOULD recommendation does not by itself require corrective action when the rule is otherwise appropriately addressed. Explain the reason and resulting customer risk in the rule response. See [migration requirements](requirements.md).

A plan's inclusion in the Mini-SDR does not establish FedRAMP approval. Completion after March 7, 2027 requires an approved plan and agreement to a FedRAMP-coordinated red team assessment. See [enforcement and deadlines](deadlines.md).
