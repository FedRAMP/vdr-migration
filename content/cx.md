# Customer Experience

!!! bug "this needs better content"

    it's intended to remind folks to consider the agency perspective

FedRAMP makes certification decisions. Each agency determines whether the risk of using a cloud service is acceptable for its own use case. A FedRAMP grace period does not change an agency's obligations or require it to accept a provider's migration timeline.

Make your migration progress information available where you currently share your certification package. Explain remaining gaps, the risk to agency data, planned corrective actions, and any steps customers can take to reduce that risk.

Respond to agency requests for information about their use of your service. Maintaining FedRAMP Certification alone may not meet an agency's needs while migration remains incomplete.
