# Enforcement and deadlines

The VDR/VER migration deadline is December 7, 2026. Services that need more time must explain the corrective actions and target timelines for each rule that still requires implementation. Include these plans in your [Mini-SDR](report.md).

| Date | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| December 7, 2026 | Services that have not fully migrated must provide per-rule corrective actions and target timelines. |
| February 3, 2027 | Services planning to complete migration after this date will be placed in “FedRAMP Certified (In Remediation)” status until they notify FedRAMP that migration is complete. |
| March 7, 2027 | The grace period ends. An extension requires FedRAMP approval of the Corrective Action Plan and agreement to a FedRAMP-coordinated red team assessment. |
| March 8, 2027 | Services that have neither fully migrated nor obtained an approved Corrective Action Plan will have their certification revoked and be banned from FedRAMP for one year. |

FedRAMP will begin working with services needing an extended conditional grace period in February. Extensions are considered case by case. A submitted plan does not by itself establish approval.

[Failure to respond to the Emergency Test](emergency-test.md#failure-to-respond) has separate consequences. Services [applying for a new Rev5 Certification](#agency-authorization-in-process) also have specific deadlines.

FedRAMP certification decisions do not determine whether an agency accepts the risk of continuing to use a service. Keep [agency customers](cx.md) informed of remaining gaps and planned corrective actions.

## Agency-provided offerings

Agency-provided offerings, including those listed in the Marketplace's “By Government, For Government” section, are exempt from FedRAMP's VDR/VER rules. They remain subject to CISA BOD 26-04. Follow your agency CIO's and CISO's instructions for implementing that directive in accordance with agency policies.

FedRAMP expects these offerings to move to an A-130 Class G Certification. Further information will be provided in a notice about the outcome of RFC-0032 Offerings By Government. The VDR/VER exemption is not conditional on declaring an intent to transition to Class G.

## Agency authorization in process

After December 7, 2026, a cloud service that receives an agency authorization to operate (ATO) must provide a VDR/VER Migration Progress Report with its application for a new FedRAMP Rev5 Certification.

If the service has not fully migrated, explain the planned corrective actions for each affected rule in the Mini-SDR. If FedRAMP approves the Corrective Action Plan, the service is eligible for a short-term certification with conditions based on that plan.

After March 7, 2027, FedRAMP will not initiate review of a service that has received an agency ATO unless it has fully migrated to VDR/VER before applying for a new Rev5 Certification.

See [what to prepare](report.md) and the [migration requirements](requirements.md).
