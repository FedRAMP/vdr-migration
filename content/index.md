# VDR/VER migration resources

Use this site to prepare for the migration to FedRAMP's Vulnerability Detection and Response (VDR) and Vulnerability Evaluation and Reporting (VER) rules and report your progress.

- [Review the migration requirements](requirements.md) and [deadlines](deadlines.md).
- [Prepare your VDR/VER Migration Progress Report](report.md), including per-rule corrective actions where needed.
- [Review the Emergency Test instructions](emergency-test.md) for the expected response window and submission process.

Services [seeking a new Rev5 Certification](deadlines.md#agency-authorization-in-process) and [agency-provided offerings](deadlines.md#agency-provided-offerings) have specific guidance. Keep [agency customers](cx.md) informed as you migrate.

The [Sources page](sources.md) links to the underlying rules and repository. Schemas, examples, and validation tools are under development; follow the Emergency Test notification for final submission requirements.
