# Migration requirements

Review each applicable rule in [Vulnerability Detection and Response](https://www.fedramp.gov/2026/reference/vulnerability-detection-and-response/) and [Vulnerability Evaluation and Reporting](https://www.fedramp.gov/2026/reference/vulnerability-evaluation-and-reporting/). Determine how it applies to your offering, implement the required practices, and document the result and remaining risk to agency customers. Meet the verification and validation requirements that apply to your offering.

## Full migration

Full migration means addressing all applicable VDR/VER rules according to their force:

- MUST and MUST NOT requirements and prohibitions must be met in full.
- SHOULD and SHOULD NOT recommendations may be addressed through implementation or a justified departure. Document the reason for a departure and assess and document the resulting risk to federal customer data.
- MAY provisions are optional. Explain your choice where useful in the report.

See [Force of the Rule](https://www.fedramp.gov/2026/rules/#force-of-the-rule) for the governing conventions. A justified SHOULD departure does not by itself require a corrective action plan when the rule is otherwise appropriately addressed.

## Reporting progress

Your [Mini-SDR](report.md) gives a brief explanation of how each rule is addressed. Individual implementation status and overall migration readiness answer different questions: an unimplemented recommendation with a justified departure can be appropriately addressed, while an implemented action may still lack required documentation or assurance.

If a required rule is not fully implemented, include its corrective action plan and timeline in that rule's response. See [corrective action plans](cap.md) and [deadlines](deadlines.md).

Keep explanations specific to your environment. Describe the security practices you use and the result they provide. For guidance on interpreting requirements in context, see [Using the Consolidated Rules](https://www.fedramp.gov/2026/rules/#its-not-a-trap).
